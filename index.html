Before you hire

Check these six things before you hire a video editor

A quick review of these six areas will tell you more than any star rating.

  1. 1

    Portfolio quality

    Look for work that's genuinely close to what you want, not just polished in general.

  2. 2

    Relevant experience

    Experience with your specific format or industry tends to reduce back-and-forth.

  3. 3

    Editing style

    Pace, colour grading, and sound design vary widely — match it to your vision.

  4. 4

    Turnaround time

    Check stated delivery windows against your own deadline before you commit.

  5. 5

    Number of revisions

    Know how many rounds of changes are included, and what happens beyond that.

  6. 6

    What's included

    Captions, colour grading, sound mixing, and exports can be extra or bundled in.
